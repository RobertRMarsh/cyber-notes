# Notes
## Controls, Frameworks, & Compliance
### Controls
- safeguards used to reduce *specific* security risks 
- used alongside frameworks to ensure that
	- goals and processes are implemented properly
	- org meets regulatory compliance
#### Examples
##### Physical Controls
- gates, fences, locks
- security guards
- CCTV, cameras, motion detectors
- access cards/badges 
##### Technical Controls
- Firewalls
- Antivirus software
- MFA
##### Administrative Controls
- separation of duties
- authorization
- asset classification
#### Control Areas
##### Encryption
- converting data from a readable format to an encoded format
- usually converting *Plaintext* to *Ciphertext*
- used to secure the Confidentiality of data
###### Ciphertext
- raw format unreadable to humans/machines
- cannot be read until decrypted to original plaintext form
##### Authentication
- process of verifying someone/something's identity
- i.e. username/password, MFA, biometrics
###### Exploits
- Vishing can steal someone's voice to use for impersonation
##### Authorization
- granting access to system resources
### Frameworks
- guidelines for making plans to mitigate risk and threats to data/privacy
#### FERC-NERC
- The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission - North American Electric Reliability Corporation
- applies to org that work with electricity/US power grid
- legally required to adhere to the CIP (Critical Infrastructure Protection) defined by FERC
#### FedRAMP
- The Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program
- aims to provide consistency across government sector and third-party cloud providers
#### CIS
- Center for Internet Security
- non profit, provides controls to use in safeguarding systems/networks from attacks
- provides actionable controls if an incident occurs
#### GDPR
- General Data Protection Regulation
- E.U regulation about their citizens privacy in and out of E.U territory
- if a business leaked a citizens data they have 72 hours to alert the citizen
#### PCI DSS
- Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
- standard for org handling credit card info/processing
- to reduce credit card fraud
#### HIPPA
- The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
- U.S based, protects citizens health info *PHI (Protected Health Info)* 
- if exposed can lead to identity theft and insurance fraud
- Also *HITRUST (Health Information Trust Alliance)* 
	- sec framework that helps institutions meet HIPPA
#### ISO
- International Organization for Standardization
- international standards for 
	- technology
	- manufacturing
	- management
#### SOC1 & SOC2
- *AICPA (American Institute of Certified Public Accountants)* developed this standard
- series of reports that focus on an organizations user access policies at different organizational levels such as
	- Associate
	- Supervisor
	- Manager
	- Executive
	- Vendor
	- Others
- Research : the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the Sarbanes-Oxley Act
#### CTF(Cyber Threat Framework)
- made by US gov to provide a common language for cyber threat activity
#### ISO/IEC 27001
- International Organization for Standardization/international Electrotechnical Commission
- 27000 family of standards manages the security of assets
