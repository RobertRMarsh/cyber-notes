### Terminology 
#### Security Frameworks
- guidelines for making plans for risk/threat mitigation
- structured approach for implementing *Security Lifecycle*
- manage different types of org and regulatory compliance risk
- purpose include
	- protecting PII and SPII
#### Security Lifestyles
- constantly evolving set of policies and procedures 
- defines how an org 
	- manages risk
	- complies with policies/law
	- follows guidelines
