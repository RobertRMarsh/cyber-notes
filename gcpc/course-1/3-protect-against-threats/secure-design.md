### Secure Design
#### CIA Triad
- Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
- Foundational to secure design for systems/ sec policies or *Asset* 
#### Asset
- Item perceived as having value to the org
- Assets can vary in importance Especially when SPII is involved
#### NIST CSF
- National Institute of Standards and Technology
	- U.S based
- Cybersecurity Framework
	- voluntary
	- consists of 
		- standards
		- guidelines
		- practices for managing risk
	- baseline to manage short and long term risk
