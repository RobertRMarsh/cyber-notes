# Notes
## Phishing Indicator types
### Phishing Tactics (Social Engineering)
- Psychological tricks used to manipulate you
- creates a sense of urgency, fear, or leverage authority
### Technical Indicator
- verifiable unforgeable flaw
- incorrect sender email domains
- malicious link addresses
### Content Indicator
- poor grammar, generic greeting, unusual request
	- less definitive but can still raise suspicious
