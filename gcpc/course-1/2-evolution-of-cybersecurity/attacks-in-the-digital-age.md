# Notes
## Attacks in the Digital Age
### LoveLetter Attack
- Made in 2000 by Onel De Guzman 
- Malware Meant to steal software
- Subject line "I Love You", attachment "Love Letter For You"   
- Scanned users address book when opened 
- Sends itself to each user on the list
- Installs a program to collect users login information and passwords
- 45 Million computers infected
- 10 Billion $ in damages
- Popularized social engineering
### Equifax Breach
- In 2017, attackers breached Equifax records
- 143 million customer records were stolen
- Roughly 40% of Americans affected
- included PII/SPII
- Result of multiple failures on Equifax part
- Several known vulnerabilities
- 575 million paid
- Popularized the cost of major breaches
