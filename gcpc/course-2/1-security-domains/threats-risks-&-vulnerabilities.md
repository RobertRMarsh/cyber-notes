# Notes
## Threats, Risks, and Vulnerabilities
### The Web's Three Layers
#### Surface
- normal websites navigated via browser
#### Deep
- intranets, requires authorization
#### Dark
- when ransom negotiations occur or data is leaked, this can be done through the dark web
- only accessed using special software
### Threats
- any even that can negatively impact assets
- example: social engineering
### Risks
- anything that can impact the *CIA* of an asset
- requires a threat + vulnerability to be a risk
- likelihood of a threat occurring 
- example: no backup protocols
- *Multiparty risk* using a third party may divulge intellectual property
- rated at diff levels
	- low risk assets = would not harm finances, rep, or operations
	- medium risk asset = not available to public and could damage finances, rep or operations 
	- high risk asset = PII, SPII and similar
### Vulnerabilities
- weakness that can be exploited via a threat
- example: outdated security devices or applications
### Three Major Impacts of All of the Above
- financial impact
- identity theft 
- organizational reputation
