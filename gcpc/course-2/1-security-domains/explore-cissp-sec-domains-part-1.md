# Notes
## Explore the 8 CISSP Security Domains, Part I
### Use case
- segmentation aids in organizing daily tasks/identifying gaps in security
- aids in setting up *Security Posture*
- *Security Posture* is an org's ability to manage defense and react to change
### The First 4 Domains
#### Security and Risk Management
- in the vein of BCP and DRP and IRP, 
##### Sub Sections
###### Defining Security Goals/Objectives
- reduces risk to critical assets, like PII
###### Risk Mitigation
- having the right procedures and rules in place to quickly reduce impact from a risk, like a breach
###### Compliance
- used to develop an orgs internal; policies, regulations, and standards
###### Business Continuity
- Maintaining everyday operations via disaster recovery plans
###### Legal Regulations
- vary worldwide
- follow rules and expectations for ethical behavior 
#### Asset Security
- securing physical and digital assets
- relates to; storage, retention, and destruction of data
- orgs need policies and procedures that ensure *Asset Security*
#### Security Architecture and Engineering
- optimizing data security through; effective tools, systems, and processes
- protect assets and data
- *Shared Responsibility* core secure design concept where: all individuals in an organization take an active role in lowering risk and maintaining security
#### Communications and Network Security
- focused on managing/securing physical networks and wireless comms
- no Bluetooth /personal hotspots
