# Notes
## NIST's Risk Management Framework
### RMF Steps
#### Prepare
- manage security/privacy risk before a breach occurs
#### Categorize 
- develop risk management processes and tasks
- the processes inform how to preform tasks and what those are to ensure the *CIA* of assets
#### Select
- Choose, customize, and capture documentation of the controls in an org
- keeping a Playbook up to date
#### Implement
- implementing security/privacy plans for org
#### Access
- determine if controls were implemented correctly
#### Authorize
- being accountable for risks in an org
- generating reports, developing plans of action, and establishing project milestones
#### Monitor
- be aware of how an org's systems operate
