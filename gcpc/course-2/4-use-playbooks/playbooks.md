# Notes
## Playbooks
- should be considered a living document
- should change as landscape changes including laws and attacks
- should outline procedures and improve over time
- used in conjunction with SIEM and SOAR tools
## Incident Response Playbook Phases 
### Preparation
- prepare to mitigate likelihood/impact of incidents
### Detection and Analysis
- detects/uses analysis to 
### Containment 
- isolate breach/incident to mitigate impact
- reduce immediate impact of incident
### Eradication and Recovery
- eliminate artifacts of the incident
- after due diligence restore systems
### Post incident activity
- lesson learned
- also incident analysis
### Coordination
- recording incidents and communicating incidents with other teams throughout an incident
