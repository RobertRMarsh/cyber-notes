# Notes
## NIST Frameworks
- National Institute of Standards and Technology
### NIST CSF(Cybersecurity Framework)
- best practices/guidelines for managing cyber risk
- expands into the protection of US fed gov with *NIST special publication*, or *SP 800-53*
#### 5 Core Functions
##### Identify
- management of cyber risk
- monitoring/identifying potential sec issues
- strong understanding of org's assets, risks, policies to est. a strong sec foundation
##### Protect
- strategy used to protect org
##### Detect
- improving monitor capabilities/ identifying potential security risks
##### Respond
- proper procedures are used to contain, neutralize, and analyze sec incidents
##### Recover
- returning affected systems to normal operations
#### New Core Function, Govern
- emphasizes importance of cybersecurity governance
- establishing and maintaining structures/processes needed to manage cyber risk
- i.e. setting clear cyber objectives, comprehensive risk management strategy, improving cyber performance
