# Notes
## Plan a Security Audit
- *Security Audits* are a review of an org's controls, policies, and procedures against expectations
- identifies org risk, access controls, and corrects compliance issues within an org
### Planning the Audit
#### Establishing the Scope/Goals
- *Scope* is specific criteria of an audit, 
	- makes org identify parts of org that might affect security posture 
- *Goals* outline of security objectives 
####  Risk Assessment of Org's Assets
- helps to understand what controls should be implemented to , mitigate risk
### Completing the Audit
#### Controls Assessment 
- are current controls enough to protect the assets
#### Assessing Compliance
- ensuring org follows relevant laws/regulations or standards
#### Communicating results to stake holders
- results and recommendations need to be communicated to stake holders
### Internal vs External Audits
### Internal 
- audit is ran by internal members of the org
#### External
- audit is ran by non-members/third party in relation to the org
